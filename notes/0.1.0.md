- **Antigravity CLI sessions on a kehikko.** A terminal holds the Antigravity CLI conversation kept for the epic, parts and kehikko you are looking at, resumed when you come back.
- **It knows where it is.** Each conversation is told its place and the modules around it, gets the kehikko's tools and skills, and is filed under its place without a command.
