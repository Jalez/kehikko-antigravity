- **Ready at its prompt from the start.** An Antigravity session that was just opened or resumed and is waiting at its prompt can be compacted or cleared, and woken by a message, without anyone typing in it first.
- **No more flicker.** Whether Compact and Clear can be pressed no longer switches off every few seconds while a session sits at its prompt.
- **A command that starts no turn no longer leaves it stuck.** After a line like `/help`, the session counts as back at its prompt after a few quiet seconds.
