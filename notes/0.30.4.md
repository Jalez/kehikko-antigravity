Kind: feature

- **Adopting an item with Antigravity no longer fails with “did not answer within 8 s”.** Antigravity now takes the task at once and starts afterwards; the dialog closes straight away and the conversation appears in the Antigravity container as it starts. Before, agy had to boot and say its conversation's id first, which could take up to a minute — far longer than Kehikot waits — and the conversation started anyway, unseen.
- **If Antigravity cannot start the task, the Inbox says why.** A conversation that is slow to say its id is no longer ended: it runs on and is filed once it does.
- **A conversation placed with /kehikot-here can now say how its turn ends.** `tell_finished` takes the conversation's key, so its Inbox line shows done, a decision for you, or research complete.
